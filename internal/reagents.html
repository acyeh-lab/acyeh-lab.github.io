Where everything in the lab is stored, and what is in each freezer box. Click a rack to open its 4×4 grid of box slots, then a slot to open that box as a 9×9 map of positions A1–I9. Click a position to see, edit, move or remove what is in it.

A box can belong to someone. Open a box on the Freezer map and put a name in Belongs to — that box then gets its own tab at the top of this page showing just their boxes, so a student can go straight to their own material and edit it without working through the whole freezer. Untagged boxes are shared lab stock.

This page and My Lab.xlsx are two views of the same data. Edit either one. A job on the cluster reconciles them every 15 minutes, so a box you fill in here appears in the spreadsheet's [Storage] tabs, and a row you type in Excel appears here. Each row carries a hidden RID column that keeps the two sides matched up — don't delete it.

The inventory is private. It is not in this public repository: it lives in acyeh-lab/lab-internal and is fetched — and written back — with the GitHub token in this browser, the same way Task Tracker works.
