Every BMT experiment sheet in the lab, indexed automatically. This page is rebuilt every morning from /fh/fast/yeh_a/grp/projects/transplant_sheets — drop a new sheet into the right project subfolder and it appears here the next day. Nothing is curated by hand, so the page can never drift from the folder.

Each experiment shows what was mined from the sheet itself: the question, the group table (with mouse counts), recipients, donors, conditioning, and the full text of every document filed under that experiment number. Companion worksheets — injection sheets, takedown sheets, antibiotic schedules — are folded in under their parent experiment.

The experiment data is private. It is not in this public repository: it lives in acyeh-lab/lab-internal and is fetched with the GitHub token in this browser, the same way Task Tracker and Journal Watch work. The Word originals stay on the lab share — each document lists its path with a Copy button.
