Something else you want to label?

A raw .annsummary.npz or .h5ad cannot be opened here — it has to be converted once on the cluster (one command, a couple of minutes): 

cd /fh/fast/yeh_a/grp/software/scseq-annotator
sbatch shell/convert.sh /path/to/IN.annsummary.npz --name MY_DATASET --embed

Writes data/MY_DATASET.bundle.json — then open that here. Ask Albert if you do not have an .annsummary.npz yet.
