CellPhoneDB — ligand–receptor interactions

There are no p-values in this tab — every number here is an expression level, not a probability. Click for what each one means and how to read it without fooling yourself.
What each number is
Mean The interaction's abundance: (x + y) / 2, where x is partner A's mean expression across the sender's cells and y is partner B's across the receiver's. Set to 0 if either side is 0. Units are the dataset's log-normalised scale (log1p of counts per 10k), so it is not a fold-change, a rate, or a probability — a bigger number means the pair is more abundantly expressed, nothing more.
% A, % B Fraction of cells in that cluster with any detected transcript of the partner — the detection rate. For a heteromer it is the lowest subunit's. These two are the only filter applied: both must exceed the threshold above.
Direction CellPhoneDB's own annotation of the pair — Ligand–Receptor, Adhesion–Adhesion, Receptor–Receptor, and so on. It describes the pair, not evidence about your data.
Class CellPhoneDB's curated pathway grouping, e.g. "Signaling by Chemokines".
Matrix cell A count of interactions passing in that direction — not a strength and not a score. Big clusters with deeper detection pass more filters, so the matrix partly tracks cluster size.
Why there are no p-values

CellPhoneDB's significance comes from a permutation test: shuffle the cell-type labels across cells 1,000 times, and the p-value is the proportion of shuffles whose mean is at least the observed one. That needs per-cell data. This page has per-cell expression for only the 25,000-cell UMAP subsample, and that subsample is drawn uniformly at random, whereas CellPhoneDB's own subsampler is deliberately heterogeneity-preserving. The Mean column, meanwhile, is computed over every cell in the cluster — so a null built from 25,000 cells would not be comparable to it, and pairing the two would inflate significance.

So this tab runs CellPhoneDB's simple-threshold mode, which its authors offer explicitly for a more permissive analysis. The thing you give up is precisely what the permutation test buys: cell-type specificity is not assessed here. A pair can top this list simply by being broadly and highly expressed in both clusters.

Reading it without fooling yourself
  1. Don't rank different interactions against each other. Comparing VEGFA→KDR to CSF1→CSF1R compares how abundant two unrelated genes are, not which matters more. The comparison that is meaningful is one interaction across cluster pairs — the same genes, different cells.
  2. Check % A and % B before believing a mean. A high mean at 12% expressing is a handful of cells; at 90% it is the population.
  3. Direction is real. X→Y is a different claim from Y→X, which is why the matrix is asymmetric. Read the sender and receiver columns, not just the pair.
  4. Absence proves nothing. Only interactions whose every subunit sits on this panel can be evaluated — see the coverage line above. A pair missing here is invisible to the panel, not absent from the tissue.
  5. Co-expression is not contact. Nothing here uses spatial coordinates; two clusters can score highly and never touch. Treat a hit as a hypothesis to check in the dot plot, the UMAP, or the image — not as a result.
  6. For a figure, run the real thing. CellPhoneDB upstream on the .h5ad gives you all cells, its own subsampling and the full database, with p-values.
